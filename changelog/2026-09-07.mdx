## New features

### Hyperliquid support in Scout

[Scout](/products/scout) now supports the Hyperliquid chain (HyperEVM). You can browse and submit contract and token entries on Hyperliquid, with the official chain logo and explorer links for addresses and transactions.

## Updates

### August juror rewards claimable in Court

The August 2026 rewards snapshots for Ethereum and Gnosis are live in [Kleros Court](/court/overview), so jurors can now claim their August staking rewards from the claim modal.

## Bug fixes

**Correct liquidity figures in Foresight** — The [Foresight](/products/foresight) app now recognizes outcome tokens with prefixed symbols when matching liquidity pools, so market liquidity totals and the homepage liquidity summary display correctly for all markets.
