## New features

### New VeaShi bridge routes

The [VeaShi](/developers/crosschain/vea-bridge) bridge now supports new routes between Ethereum, Arbitrum, and three additional chains:

- **Robinhood** (chain ID 4217) — routes to and from Ethereum and Arbitrum.
- **Tempo** (chain ID 4663) — routes to and from Ethereum and Arbitrum, plus a Sepolia testnet route.
- **ARC** (chain ID 5042) — routes to and from Ethereum and Arbitrum.

Route addresses are published in the `@kleros/veashi-sdk` package on npm. See the [VeaShi deployment addresses](/developers/crosschain/vea-deployment-addresses) page for the full list of supported routes.
