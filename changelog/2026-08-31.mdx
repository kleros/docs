## New features

### Vote tallies and turnout in Court round history

The round history on [Kleros Court](/court/overview) case pages now shows how each round was decided. Once voting in a round is final, you can see the vote count for each ruling option, including reserved options like "Refuse to arbitrate", along with a turnout summary of how many jurors voted. If counts aren't available for a dispute, the round history displays gracefully without them.

## Updates

### Round navigation always available in Court

Round buttons in the [Kleros Court](/court/overview) case round history now stay clickable even when jurors submitted no justifications, so you can browse every round of a dispute. Long ruling option labels are also truncated cleanly for better readability.
