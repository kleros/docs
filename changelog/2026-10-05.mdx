## New features

### Verified Reality.eth answer options in Court

[Kleros Court](/court/overview) now looks up [Reality.eth](/products/reality) questions and their templates on-chain to build the answer options for these cases. It no longer relies on the arbitrable's display script. This stops crafted question parameters from changing the options jurors see.

Court also shows a notice on the case page when:

- Other interfaces display different answer options. Vote according to the options shown in Court.
- The question parameters are malformed, or the options could not be verified or determined. Read the question carefully. If in doubt, refuse to arbitrate.

### Notional volume breakdown in Foresight

The [Foresight](/products/foresight) header now shows the notional volume across markets. Click it to open a breakdown by market and by outcome, in sDAI and USD.

## Updates

### USD values in the Foresight header

Total liquidity in the [Foresight](/products/foresight) header now displays in USD, matching volume. Both values wait for the sDAI price before they appear. Chart legend prices now show two decimals.

### Reality.eth library updates

Dispute Resolver, the Kleros SDK, and the [Reality.eth cross-chain proxies](/products/reality) now use updated Reality.eth libraries.

## Bug fixes

**VeaShi Arbitrum to Tempo route** — The Arbitrum to Tempo route in `@kleros/veashi-sdk` now points to the correct address. See [VeaShi deployment addresses](/developers/crosschain/vea-deployment-addresses).
