This guide helps you pick the right Kleros product and points you to the docs page that explains how to set it up. Find your case in the table, read the short section, then follow the links.

**What the statuses mean:**

- **Supported:** works today, maintained, and you can set it up without the Kleros team.
- **Supported in the browser:** the browser app is self-serve. Connecting your own app or contract needs the Kleros team to whitelist it.
- **Will be deprecated soon:** works today but is being phased out. Don't start long-term work on it.
- **Not encouraged:** works, but Kleros advises against new integrations. Use only an existing Kleros proxy with its standard policy.
- **Not recommended:** there is no self-serve route today.

## How a Kleros case works

- **Court:** each court has its own rules (policy), fee per juror, and timings. If none fits, use the General Court.
- **Policy:** the written rules jurors follow. On most paths you write it yourself, so make it clear. Guide: [Policy writing guide](/legacy/integrate/policy-guide)
- **Evidence:** cases and evidence are public. Have your evidence ready before you open a case.
- **Ruling:** 1 is your first option, 2 your second, and so on. 0 means jurors refused or the vote tied.
- **Appeal:** anyone can fund an appeal after the vote. The next round has more jurors and costs more.
- **Cost:** fee per juror × number of jurors, plus gas.

## Recognition of Jurisdiction

Your terms of service name Kleros Court for your disputes. You open each case in the Dispute Resolver at [resolve.kleros.io](https://resolve.kleros.io), jurors decide, and you carry out the result. No contracts needed.

**Networks:** Ethereum for high-value cases (over $5k) and Gnosis for lower-value cases.

**Good fit if:** you can apply the outcome yourself (refund, payout, restore an account), and your platform is off-chain or on a chain Kleros isn't on.

**Cost:** 3 jurors in the General Court cost 0.0162 ETH on Ethereum or 36 xDAI on Gnosis. A round takes about 8 to 15 days on Ethereum.

How to set it up:

- [Dispute Resolver and Recognition of Jurisdiction](/legacy/integrate/dispute-resolver)
- [Writing your dispute policy](/legacy/integrate/policy-guide)

## Proof of Humanity

Check whether a wallet belongs to one registered human, for example to give one reward per person. Users register in the PoH app, and your app reads their status from a contract.

**Networks:** Ethereum and Gnosis.

How to set it up:

- [Integration guide](/developers/products/poh/integration)
- [How users register](/tutorials/poh-register-and-vouch)

<Tip>
Read `CrossChainProofOfHumanity` on Ethereum (`0xa478095886659168E8812154fB0DE39F103E74b2`) and Gnosis (`0x16044E1063C08670f8653055A786b7CC2034d2b0`). A wallet counts as human if either chain returns true. To count each person once, store rewards by the humanity ID from `humanityOf(address)`, not by wallet.
</Tip>

## Scout

Kleros-curated data about contracts and tokens: labels, token details, and which website a contract belongs to. You can read the data or add your own entries.

**Networks:** the Scout registries are on Gnosis. Entries describe contracts on many chains.

How to set it up:

- [Scout website](https://scout-app.kleros.io/home): look up entries without code
- [Scout for developers](/developers/products/scout/overview): API, token list, registries
- [Scout Snap for MetaMask](/products/scout-snap)
- [Adding entries](/tutorials/scout-tutorial)

Kleros has not published rate limits for the Scout API. We recommend batching requests and caching results on your server rather than calling the API from each user's browser.

## Small value disputes: Court V2 Beta and V2 Agentic

Open a case in your browser with the Court V2 Dispute Resolver, jurors rule, and you apply the ruling. V2 Agentic is the Agentic Commerce Court (court 34), where jurors are expected to be AI agents.

**Networks:** Arbitrum One.

<Warning>
Opening cases in the browser needs no approval. Connecting your own app or contract to Court V2 needs the Kleros team to whitelist it, and Kleros is not currently promoting web3 integrations on V2 as it is still in Beta and technical parameters will change.
</Warning>

**Good fit if:** each dispute is for testing or worth below $1,000, you can apply the ruling yourself (refund, release a payment, remove a listing), and the case can be judged from written material.

Costs for 3 jurors:

| Court | Cost | Time without appeal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agentic Commerce Court (34) | 0.00081 ETH (about 2 USD) | Up to about 37 hours. The evidence period is only 10 minutes. |
| General Court (1) | 0.015 ETH | Up to about 21 days |
| Commerce Court (33) | 0.054 ETH | Up to about 21 days |

How to set it up:

- [Open a case](https://v2.kleros.builders/#/resolver)
- [Dispute Resolver docs](/developers/products/dispute-resolver/overview)
- [Court V2 overview](/developers/products/court/overview)

## AI agents

Tools that help AI agents read Kleros data and build with it.

- **Kleros docs for agents:** connect an agent to the Kleros docs MCP server at `https://docs.kleros.io/mcp`. [Setup for Claude Code, Cursor and others](/ai-tools/mcp-server). A plain-text index is at [docs.kleros.io/llms.txt](https://docs.kleros.io/llms.txt).
- **Read courts and disputes:** `@kleros/agentkit` is a read-only command-line tool and MCP server for courts, disputes, evidence and policies on Court V1 and V2. Try `npx -y @kleros/agentkit dispute get <disputeID> --chain arbitrum-one`.
- **Kleros skills:** ready-made instructions for agents, including Curate: [github.com/kleros/kleros-skills](https://github.com/kleros/kleros-skills)

## Existing or legacy integrations

These paths work, but don't start new long-term work on them: some might be deprecated, and others need help from the Kleros team.

### Escrow V1 (will be deprecated soon)

Holds a payment between a buyer and a seller. If they disagree, Kleros jurors decide and the contract pays out. The Kleros Escrow front end will be deprecated soon and will not be maintained for long.

- [Escrow overview](/developers/products/escrow/overview)
- [Step-by-step tutorial](/tutorials/escrow-tutorial)

### Reality.eth (not encouraged)

Ask a question on Reality.eth. People answer by posting bonds, and if an answer is disputed, Kleros jurors decide. If you still use it, pick an existing Kleros proxy from the list below and use its standard policy. Check the arbitrator address against the list. Don't rely on the name in the Reality.eth app menu, because some entries there are old. A custom proxy or policy needs the Kleros team.

- [Kleros proxies (addresses and networks)](/developers/products/reality/smart-contracts#kleros-arbitrator-proxy-addresses)
- [Integration docs](/developers/products/reality/integration)

### Your own smart contract (not recommended)

Kleros does not recommend writing your own arbitrable contract for now, because Kleros will transition to V2, where contracts need whitelisting (see [Small value disputes](#small-value-disputes-court-v2-beta-and-v2-agentic)). Use one of the paths above.

- For background only: [Arbitrable apps overview](/developers/arbitrable-apps/arbitrable-overview)

## When to contact Kleros

Only for:

- Whitelisting your app or contract on Court V2.
- A custom Reality.eth proxy or policy.

Everything else in this guide is self-serve.

When you write, include:

- Links to your GitHub and socials.
- What you want to build and which path above you considered.
- The chain you are on.
- How many disputes or requests you expect per year, and the amounts involved.
- Whether rulings must be enforced automatically by a contract.
- Your planned launch date.
