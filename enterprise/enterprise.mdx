**Kleros Enterprise is online dispute resolution for enterprises, governments and financial institutions.** Organizations use it to resolve complaints from customers or citizens quickly, at low cost, and through a demonstrably impartial process.

When a dispute is submitted, a panel of independent evaluators reviews the evidence from both sides and applies a pre-agreed ruleset, the [Dispute Policy](/enterprise/how-it-works#the-dispute-policy), to reach a decision. The process runs entirely online and typically concludes in days. Neither the organization nor its users need to handle crypto or interact with smart contracts.

<Note>
Kleros is designed for low and medium-value disputes where speed, cost and perceived fairness matter most. It is **not** a replacement for courts, formal legal systems or legal counsel. It adds a fast, online option that operates before or alongside those channels.
</Note>

## What Kleros Is and Is Not

| Kleros is | Kleros is not |
| --- | --- |
| An online platform for resolving low and medium-value disputes | A replacement for courts or formal legal systems |
| A neutral, independent layer added to an organization's existing complaint process | A legal services provider |
| A mechanism that operates before courts or regulators become involved | An internal complaint handling team |
| Adaptable to the rules and jurisdiction of each organization | A platform for high-complexity disputes or criminal matters |

## Web3 and DAO Integration

Kleros also works natively with Web3 and DAO ecosystems. Disputes in decentralized communities are resolved without centralized intermediaries, with rulings enforced automatically by smart contracts: protocol governance disputes, on-chain moderation, oracle disputes and rule enforcement on decentralized platforms. See the [Ecosystem page](/enterprise/ecosystem) for projects building on Kleros.
