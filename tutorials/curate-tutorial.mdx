# Curate Tutorial

<Warning>
**Classic Curate is retired in the Curate app.** You can no longer browse, submit to, or create Classic lists in the [Curate app](https://curate.kleros.io). A Classic list's address now opens a **Classic lists are retired** page that links to the contract on the block explorer. The Classic Curate contracts stay deployed on chain, with their entries and history. **Light Curate and Stake Curate are not affected.** The Curate app calls Light Curate lists **Standard lists**, and only Standard and Stake lists can be Kleros Verified.
</Warning>

This tutorial covers creating Kleros Curate V1 lists and submitting and challenging items on them. It uses Light Curate, which the [Curate app](https://curate.kleros.io) calls **Standard lists**.

Curate lists are deployed as smart contracts. The platform uses a hierarchical structure where top-level lists can contain sublists.

---

## Create a List

<Note>
The Curate app creates Standard (Light Curate) and Stake lists only. Classic lists can no longer be created. For Stake lists, see [Stake Curate](/products/stake-curate).
</Note>

Click **Launch a list** in the Curate app, then choose a Standard list to open the list wizard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="List">
    - **Name**: the name of your list (for example, "Hip Hop Legends")
    - **Description**: a brief overview of the list's purpose
    - **List logo**: the image shown with your list
    - **Listing policy (PDF)**: the acceptance criteria specifying what submissions are accepted or rejected
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    Set the **Submission deposit** and choose the **Court** and the **Jurors in the first round** based on the list's complexity and value. Under **Advanced options**, you can set each deposit individually (submission, removal, submission challenge, and removal challenge), the challenge period, the governor, and the appeal stakes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Item">
    Add the fields for submissions (text, images, and other types) and mark the fields that identify an entry as **Identifier**. Set the item name, the label for individual submissions (for example, "Hip Hop Legend").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Deploy the list with one transaction from your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Register in the List of Lists

<Note>
The Kleros lists of lists run on Light Curate, and only the lists registered on them are Kleros Verified. On November 17, 2026, Kleros replaced its Gnosis Chain list of lists with a new one at `0x5f688a4bac5e2787ef1d9de325831e537ee4cffa`. The former one (`0x2418d887d0d0fe03b793a6af4321fa488226a0b5`) no longer grants Kleros Verified, so submit new Gnosis lists to the new address.
</Note>

After the deploy, click **Submit it to** followed by the list of lists' name. The Curate app opens the list of lists' submit page with your new list filled in, where you submit it and pay the deposit. Once your list is registered on the list of lists, it is Kleros Verified on the Curate app.

---

## Submit & Challenge Items

- **Submissions**: users deposit funds to add items to a list. The deposit is returned if the item is accepted.
- **Challenges**: anyone can dispute a submission by describing how it violates the list's policy. Kleros jurors arbitrate the dispute, and the winner receives the loser's deposit (minus arbitration fees).

<Tip>
Write your acceptance criteria carefully before deploying. Jurors rule on challenges based on your list's policy, so ambiguous criteria lead to unpredictable outcomes.
</Tip>
